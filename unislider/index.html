What you are judging. Each trial shows two versions, A and B, of the same editing slider: the same edit rendered at every strength, in two different ways. They start paused at the lowest strength; press Play to sweep from low to high strength and back, or step to any strength to look closely.

The row of cells under the images is the strength axis. White means no visible difference between A and B at that strength; orange to dark plum means a growing difference, on the same scale for every trial. Triangles mark this pair's three most different strengths. Click a cell to jump there.

Answer three questions. Each starts at Same; change it only where you see a difference.

  • Smoothness / Consistency: which slider changes more smoothly and consistently as you move it, with no flicker or jumps in parts that should stay the same?
  • Fewer artifacts: which shows fewer visual defects: double edges, see-through or doubled objects, washed-out or mixed regions, smears, blur, distorted shapes.
  • Better overall: which slider you would rather use.
  • Keys: Space play or pause; ← → step the strength; F flicker A and B in one place; O flicker both with the original image; hover for a 2× lens.
  • Answers: Q W E smoothness / consistency A / same / B; A S D artifacts; Z X C overall; Enter next; Backspace back.
  • This is one of three parts.

Tips
  • Slide through strengths with the arrow keys (← →) to look at smoothness and consistency.
  • A/B flicker (F) might be helpful for revealing small differences.
  • Colour shows how different the two sliders are across strengths.
